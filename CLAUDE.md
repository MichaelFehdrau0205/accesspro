# Access Pro

Work in this folder. The product is Access Pro: a person sets access needs once, picks a situation, and gets a ready-to-send request plus a high-contrast access card to show staff.

## Product rules

- Free for the individual. No login in the first version. Save the profile locally.
- The access card is the main screen. It must be readable at arm’s length, high contrast, and usable with no talking.
- Situations to support first: doctor, housing office, court, job interview. 911, transit, and events can wait.
- Needs to support: ASL interpreter, captions, visual alerts, wheelchair access, plain language, quiet space.
- One generation step writes both the request text and the card text.
- Do not add accounts, history, maps, or extra languages until the card flow works.

## Tone

Plain language. Short sentences. The card speaks for the person, for example: “I’m Deaf. Please use written notes or text. I need an ASL interpreter.”
